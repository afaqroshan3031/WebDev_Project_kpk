About John O'Reilly

Hi, I'm John O'Reilly. I've been around cars for as long as I can remember — fixing them, driving them, and sometimes arguing with them.

Wheels on Fire is my way of helping people find a car that actually suits them. Not the flashiest one, not the cheapest one — just the right one.

I'm a bit of a perfectionist with engines (and maybe with people too). Expect honest opinions and the occasional dry comment. No pressure, no nonsense.

Why choose us?

  • Honest advice — no sales talk
  • Carefully chosen cars
  • Real experience, not just talk
